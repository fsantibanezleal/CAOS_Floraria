## Digitized blooms

Selected scans originate from Smithsonian Gardens and the Smithsonian 3D/Open Access program:

- Phalaenopsis amabilis.
- Encyclia tampensis var. alba.
- Lycaste virginalis.
- Phragmipedium x richteri 'Wolf River'.
- Vanda coerulea.

Each has compact/detailed variants. The catalog retains individual credit; the source lock records exact documents, URLs and hashes.

[Smithsonian 3D orchids](https://gardens.si.edu/exhibitions/future-of-orchids/3d-orchids/) and [Open Access](https://www.si.edu/openaccess/faq) supply context; item-specific CC0 evidence is authoritative.
