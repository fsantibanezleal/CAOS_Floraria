# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and focused on the work. Welcome questions from different botanical, language and technical backgrounds. Support corrections with evidence and acknowledge uncertainty.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing and publication of private information are unacceptable. Do not claim endorsement without authorization.

Report concerns privately through the contact channel on [the author's website](https://fsantibanezleal.github.io). Share only necessary information. The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation proportionately while respecting privacy.

This applies to project discussion and review. Clear evidence-based technical criticism is welcome.
