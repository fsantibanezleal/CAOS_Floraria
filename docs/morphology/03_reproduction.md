# Reproduction and structural identities

The lifecycle follows an explicitly successful explanatory route. Transfer to a stigma differs from later fertilization. Keep the gap visible: a particle's arrival calculates neither compatibility nor seed probability.

A pollen-tube diagram connects named structures at readable scale. Its path/speed are authored. Progress is ordinal; a midpoint is neither half the natural duration nor a 50 percent success probability.

Track the ovule toward seed and the ovary's contribution toward fruit. Preserve identity rather than treating the last object as a replacement for the flower. Some fruits include other floral tissue; a general diagram cannot be assigned to every edible structure.

Orchid seeds limit generalization: an endosperm-rich bean-like diagram is not a universal orchid model. FLORARIA does not claim orchid germination or simulate a protocorm. Later establishment/growth requires separate evidence.

Pause/step through written explanations without continuous motion. Educational usefulness must not be reported as measured learning without a study.
